### 🧠 About Me

I sit at an unusual intersection: **civil engineering** taught me to design systems that hold people up, and **software engineering** lets me build systems that scale to them. I care about infrastructure — the physical kind that connects cities, and the digital kind that connects people who've been left out of the conversation.

- 🔭 Currently building **[SignVrse](https://signvrse.com)** — an AI-powered accessibility platform translating sign language at scale
- 🌱 Going deep on **Rust**, **Machine Learning**, and **Python** internals
- 🛠️ Comfortable shipping in **Next.js, React, Spring Boot, React Native**
- 🤝 Open to collaborating on **systems programming, ML/AI, and civil engineering tech**
- 💬 Ask me about **NEXT, REACT, SPRING BOOT, ML/AI, or structural design**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I think reinforced concrete and gradient descent have more in common than people realize
